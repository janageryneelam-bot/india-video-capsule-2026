Content & Video Guidelines

What can be submitted to the Capsule.

Last updated: [INSERT DATE]

1. Video specifications

  • Length: 3–5 seconds.
  • Format: MP4 or WebM.
  • Maximum size: 5 MB per file.
  • Submit only files you have permission to use.
  • Where two options are requested, the final version must be confirmed before publication.

2. Rights and permissions

You must own the content or have permission to use it, including music, footage, artwork, logos, photographs and the likenesses of identifiable people. Obtain appropriate consent from people shown, and parental or guardian permission for children.

3. Accuracy and authenticity

Descriptions, achievements, credentials, business claims and destination links must be accurate and not misleading. We may request clarification or supporting information before approving a submission.

4. Content that may be rejected

  • Illegal, infringing, fraudulent, impersonating or deceptive content.
  • Harassment, threats, hateful abuse or explicit material unsuitable for a general public archive.
  • Private information shared without permission.
  • Malware, deceptive links, phishing or unsafe destinations.
  • Content that violates another person's rights or applicable law.

5. Review and replacement

Every entry is subject to manual review. We aim to review within two business days. If offered, the replacement window is seven business days after initial approval, with up to three replacement attempts. The current approved version may remain live while a replacement is reviewed.

6. Destination links

Provide a URL you are authorised to share. We may reject or disable links that are unsafe, broken, misleading or inconsistent with the Capsule's purpose. We cannot guarantee that an external website will remain available.

7. Report a concern

Contact [indiavideocapsule2026@gmail.com] with the entry serial number or URL, details of the concern and relevant supporting information. We may temporarily restrict content while reviewing a credible complaint.
