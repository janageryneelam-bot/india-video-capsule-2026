1. Before you pay

Review your category, price, destination URL and submission requirements before payment. Enterprise / Corporate Giant pricing will be confirmed in writing before you pay.

2. If a submission needs correction

If a submission does not meet the guidelines, we may ask you to correct it or submit a replacement within a reasonable time. Payment alone does not guarantee approval or publication.

3. If we cannot approve your entry

The participant may request a correction or replacement during the seven-business-day window described below. If we reject an entry and cannot provide the paid service, we will explain the available remedy. Nothing in this policy limits refunds or remedies required by applicable law.

4. Seven-business-day change and cancellation window

The seven-business-day window begins on the date your entry is first approved and published. We will communicate the approval/publication date and, where practical, the final date for requests. Within this window, you may request video replacements, corrections to entry details, an early lock of your entry, or a refund. Video replacements remain subject to review and the stated replacement limits.

After the seven-business-day window closes, the entry is locked for the remainder of 2026. Ordinary edits, voluntary cancellations and refund requests will not ordinarily be accepted after that point, except where required by applicable law, where a duplicate/failed payment must be corrected, or where we have not provided the paid service as promised. A request made within the window is not automatically approved; we will confirm the outcome.

5. Duplicate or failed payments

If you believe you were charged twice or payment succeeded without a corresponding order, contact us with the transaction reference. We will investigate and arrange an appropriate correction or refund where due.

6. Refund processing

Approved refunds will be initiated through the original payment method or the payment provider's supported process. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and bank. We will communicate the status where reasonably possible. This policy does not remove any non-waivable consumer rights.
