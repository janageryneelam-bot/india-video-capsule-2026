Permanent Archive Policy

What the 31 December 2026 freeze means—and what it cannot promise.

Important: “Permanent” describes the Capsule's preservation goal, not a guarantee that a website or file can remain online forever. Publish only after your hosting, backup, funding and takedown processes are decided.

1. Purpose

The Capsule is designed to present a curated snapshot of selected moments associated with 2026.

2. Freeze date

The planned freeze deadline is 31 December 2026 at 10:00 PM India Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30). By this time, new entries and ordinary edits are intended to close, and approved entries will be placed in the final frozen state of the 2026 archive, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

3. What preservation means

Our goal is to retain approved entry media and metadata and keep the archive publicly accessible. The exact preservation method depends on the hosting, storage, backup and operational arrangements actually in place. We will not describe the archive as decentralised, cryptographically verified or backed by a particular CDN unless those features have been implemented and verified.

4. Availability and third-party links

We cannot guarantee uninterrupted uptime, indefinite hosting, or that a participant's external website, social profile or product page will continue to work. We may change hosting providers or technical systems to maintain the service.

5. Corrections and exceptional removal

Although the archive is intended to be frozen, entries may need correction, restriction or removal to address legal obligations, privacy requests, rights complaints, security issues, serious inaccuracies or other compelling circumstances. The freeze is not a promise to disregard the law or valid rights.

6. No recurring participant fee

Unless clearly disclosed before purchase, participants are not charged a recurring fee by the Capsule for the entry they purchased. This does not constitute a guarantee of perpetual hosting or unlimited future services.

7. Future exhibitions or commercial arrangements

The Organiser may consider future exhibitions or partnerships. No resale value, auction proceeds, royalties or participant revenue share is promised unless agreed separately in writing.

8. Sale or transfer of the Capsule

The Organiser may sell or transfer the Capsule project or relevant business assets to a successor operator. Any transfer of approved content permissions is limited to what is reasonably necessary to continue operating, preserving, displaying and promoting the existing archive, subject to the Terms & Conditions and applicable law. Participants retain ownership of their underlying videos and intellectual property. A transfer does not automatically permit standalone resale of a participant's video or unrelated commercial use.
